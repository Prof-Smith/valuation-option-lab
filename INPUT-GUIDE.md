# Student Input Guide

Students edit CSV values only. Do not edit the workflow, dashboard JavaScript, CRR function, or validation assertions.

- Enter rates and volatility as decimals, such as `0.045` and `0.28`.
- Use exactly `call` or `put`, and exactly `european` or `american`.
- Use at least five analyst targets with one common horizon.
- Ensure bid does not exceed ask and all targets are positive.
- Document every material input with a date, source, and method note.
